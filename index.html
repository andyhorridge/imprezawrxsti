THE CAR

2004 Subaru Impreza WRX STI

A Japanese-market Subaru Impreza WRX STI developed as a complete performance package, combining substantial Roger Clark Motorsport engine and drivetrain upgrades with Nitron, Whiteline and AP Racing chassis components.

Alongside the modifications, considerable attention has been given to specialist servicing, preventative maintenance and long-term preservation.
